\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
% =============================================================================
The completed result table contains one real baseline and 480 complex configurations. All values in this section are regenerated from the supplied experiment CSV. Because the same fixed patient test cohort is used for the entire architecture grid, the factor-level and top-configuration summaries are descriptive.

\subsection{Overall Factorial Performance}
The real magnitude baseline achieved test AUC \RealAUC and AP \RealAP. Across the 480 complex configurations, AUC ranged from \ComplexMinAUC to \BestAUC, with mean AUC \ComplexMeanAUC and mean AP \ComplexMeanAP. \ComplexWins/\ComplexTotal complex configurations exceeded the real-baseline AUC.

\subsection{Input-Domain Summary}
Image-domain complex models had mean AUC \ImageAUC and mean AP \ImageAP. Fourier-domain complex models had mean AUC \FourierAUC and mean AP \FourierAP. The input-domain factor contains 240 configurations in each level, so these means average over identical numbers of pooling, normalization, convolution, activation, and stream/interaction combinations.

For normalization, complex BatchNorm averaged AUC \ImageBatchnormAUC in image space and \KspaceBatchnormAUC in Fourier space; RMSNorm averaged \ImageRmsAUC and \KspaceRmsAUC, respectively. For the five stream/interaction levels, the image-domain means were \ImageComplexOnlyNoneAUC (complex-only/none), \ImageComplexOnlyHolographicAUC (complex-only/holographic), \ImageDualNoneAUC (dual/none), \ImageDualModulusGateAUC (dual/modulus-gate), and \ImageDualHolographicAUC (dual/holographic). The corresponding Fourier-domain means were \KspaceComplexOnlyNoneAUC, \KspaceComplexOnlyHolographicAUC, \KspaceDualNoneAUC, \KspaceDualModulusGateAUC, and \KspaceDualHolographicAUC.

Standard convolution averaged AUC \ConvStandardAUC versus \ConvWidelyLinearAUC for widely-linear convolution. The activation means were \ActModreluAUC for modReLU, \ActMagnitudeSiluAUC for magnitude-gated SiLU, \ActCreluAUC for CReLU, and \ActCardioidAUC for cardioid.
